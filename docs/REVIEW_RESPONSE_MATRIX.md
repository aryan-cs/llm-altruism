## Verified venue requirements

- NeurIPS 2026 limits main text to nine content pages, including figures and tables; references, appendices, and the checklist are excluded from that limit.
- The E&D Track uses the default double-blind `\usepackage[eandd]{neurips_2026}` format.
- Benchmark code must be accessible, documented, executable, and anonymous at submission time.
- A paper that introduces new datasets must provide accessible hosting plus valid Croissant core and Responsible AI metadata. The benchmark/environment-only case still requires the code-hosting policy.
- The official checklist must follow the references and optional appendix and retain its questions, answer macros, and justifications.

Authoritative sources: [NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook), [E&D Call for Papers](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets), [E&D FAQ](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsFAQ), and [Paper Checklist Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist).

## Precision design gate

Before any confirmatory campaign, the experiment manifest must freeze:

1. the primary estimand for each part and its unit of independent replication;
2. minimum and maximum sample sizes;
3. target 95% confidence-interval half-widths;
4. stopping rules that depend only on precision and protocol validity, never on effect direction or significance;
5. prompt/source deduplication and stratification rules;
6. the seed schedule and generation controls;
7. the treatment of truncation, extraction failure, provider failure, and missing output;
8. family-aware influence analyses and the limits of vendor-level generalization.

No result may be promoted by cherry-picking seeds, omitting failed routes, pooling dependent rows as independent evidence, or changing the protocol after inspecting model rankings.
