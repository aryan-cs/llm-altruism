## Verified venue requirements

- NeurIPS 2026 limits main text to nine content pages, including figures and tables; references, appendices, and the checklist are excluded from that limit.
- The E&D Track uses the default double-blind `\usepackage[eandd]{neurips_2026}` format.
- Benchmark code must be accessible, documented, executable, and anonymous at submission time.
- A paper that introduces new datasets must provide accessible hosting plus valid Croissant core and Responsible AI metadata. An executable environment/codebase without a static dataset does not need dataset hosting, while submission-time code release is required when the primary contribution is a reusable executable artifact.
- The official checklist must follow the references and optional appendix and retain its questions, answer macros, and justifications.
- The submission is one anonymous PDF of at most 50 MB plus at most one separate anonymous code/data ZIP of at most 100 MB; acknowledgments and identity-leaking external links are forbidden during double-blind review.
- Required E&D data/code had to be accessible to reviewers in final form by May 6, 2026 AoE. Private preview or gated dataset URLs are allowed during review; accepted code/data must be public by camera ready.
- Dataset submissions must provide the reviewer-accessible URL and validated Croissant metadata in OpenReview; dataset collections and processed datasets must satisfy the per-dataset hosting/metadata guidance.

Authoritative sources: [NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook), [E&D Call for Papers](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets), [E&D FAQ](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsFAQ), [E&D Hosting Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsHosting), and [Paper Checklist Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist).

## Precision design gate

Before any confirmatory campaign, the experiment manifest must freeze:

1. the primary estimand for each part and its unit of independent replication;
2. the exact fixed sample size and cell count for every part;
3. the planning assumptions and 95% interval half-width diagnostics that justify those fixed counts;
4. the prohibition on outcome-adaptive stopping, with quarantine triggered only by prespecified protocol or route failures;
5. prompt/source deduplication and stratification rules;
6. the seed schedule and generation controls;
7. the treatment of truncation, extraction failure, provider failure, and missing output;
8. family-aware influence analyses and the limits of vendor-level generalization.

No result may be promoted by cherry-picking seeds, omitting failed routes, pooling dependent rows as independent evidence, or changing the protocol after inspecting model rankings.
